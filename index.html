About Arjun Estates

Our Story

Two Decades of Building Excellence

Arjun Estates & Builders was founded in 2002 by Mr. Arjun Kumar Sharma with a simple mission: to make quality construction accessible to the middle and upper-middle class families of Delhi NCR. Starting with a single builder floor project in Lajpat Nagar, the company has grown to become one of the region's most trusted names in real estate.

Today, we operate across 15 cities in the NCR region, with a team of 120+ professionals including architects, civil engineers, interior designers, and real estate advisors. Our portfolio spans builder floors, independent villas, commercial complexes, and large residential townships.

From the Founder

A Message from Our Founder

"When I started Arjun Estates in 2002 with my life savings and a rented office in Lajpat Nagar, I made a promise to myself and to my first client: I will never compromise on quality, and I will never lie about a property. That promise has been the foundation of everything we've built — not just homes, but relationships, trust, and a reputation that no marketing budget could buy."

Twenty-two years later, I'm proud to say that 70% of our business still comes from referrals and repeat clients. That, to me, is the truest measure of success in real estate. We are not just builders — we are custodians of the life savings of middle-class Indian families, and we take that responsibility with the utmost seriousness.

Arjun Kumar Sharma
Founder & Managing Director
